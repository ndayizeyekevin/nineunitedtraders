Phone country picker data

Country and territory names are derived from mledoze/countries:
https://github.com/mledoze/countries
The source dataset is licensed under ODbL-1.0. Its license is included in
licenses/odbl-1.0.txt.

International calling codes are derived from Google libphonenumber:
https://github.com/google/libphonenumber
The source project is licensed under Apache-2.0. Its license is included in
licenses/apache-2.0.txt.

Country flag SVGs are provided by flag-icons v7.2.3:
https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons
The flag-icons MIT license is included in ../images/flags/LICENSE.
